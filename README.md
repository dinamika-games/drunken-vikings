# Drunken Vikings

![A viking rows a small boat behind the wedding longship and picks up the treats which fall into the water, while the camera in the corner shows the player rowing, dressed up as a viking](docs/gameplay.gif)

**Drunken Vikings is a rowing game for children, played by rowing in front of a webcam: you row, and so you train your abs.**

There are three ways to play:

* **Party**, for the smaller children: row behind the wedding longship and pick up the flower wreaths, honey cakes and apples which fall into the water. There is nothing to lose.
* **Chase**: row to the next town before the pirates catch you. Keep rowing, and you make it.
* **The farm**: trade goods between the settlements, help the people of the lakes with their tasks, and look after your farm.

![Two vikings sit on the floor in front of a laptop with a webcam and row with resistance bands around their feet](docs/how_to_play.jpg)

Put the laptop on the floor and sit on the floor in front of it, facing the webcam, as the two vikings in the picture do.
You can row alone or together with a friend: then each of you pulls the oar on your side, and you turn the boat by pulling harder than the other.
Row best with a Theraband or another rubber band around your feet and in your hands: it resists your pull, so your strokes are slower and better controlled.

**Keep your back straight, do not slouch, and make no sudden movements, so that you do not hurt yourself!**

**No network access.**
The game runs entirely on your computer and works offline.
The webcam images are processed locally, in memory, and are never sent anywhere.
